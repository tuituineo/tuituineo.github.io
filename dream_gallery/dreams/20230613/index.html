June 13, 2023 – Improv, Gaëlle, and Performance

...I was at an improv show with Gaëlle (theater teacher), Angèle (girl from middle school), Clara (from improv), and a man. We were in a small cabaret-style room and had to perform improv scenes. I played a boxing match where I lost but still shone. Gaëlle then came on stage as a bird, and no one wanted to go help her on stage. At the end of the show, I took pictures with my camera, and a woman told me, "That was great! Why not do the next one?"
