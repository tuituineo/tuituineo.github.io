I was at the pool. I saw the tidebs getting ready for a one-week trip where they had to dress well at all times (even in pajamas). Maël was there too, dressed very nicely, with a denim bertel. Personally, I was just hungry, and I headed to a bar.

~

I found myself in a sort of schoolyard, still inside the hotel, and I knew it was a festive day. But it was hiding a bigger secret. Gunther the penguin was going to take over the world, and for that, he needed a specific object. A kind of statuette. I followed him through the crowd and saw him go through a door. When his back was turned, I saw two girls who had the object. I got next to them and managed to get the object. I started running until a "human wall made of kids and teenagers" "clipped" into me, horizontally, around the line above my chest. It hurt a lot, and the kids looked at me as if to say, "Sorry, but you asked for it." I walked in pain until I reached a one-person elevator, where I squeezed myself in. I could feel it was a one-cable elevator, and when I moved the cabin, everything shifted. Not understanding the buttons, I got out and a tall Black man came, pressed buttons outside and inside, and was able to go down. I retraced my steps and saw Gunther get the object he needed, in slow motion. I think I still had time to run and stop him from getting it, even if I was quite far. But I got discouraged. Gunther got the object, which turned out to be a crown, and everyone fell to the ground as the building was covered in shadow. The world was under his control. "Your souls belong to me," a deep voice said. I felt disheartened. I knew there was still hope because there was a solution, but I was exhausted.

I had decided to flee.

~

I was then looking for an exit through different hallways. I eventually reached a sort of lobby, with people coming and going. I wanted to take the door, but an invisible wall blocked me. I felt even sadder and more hopeless. I hit an NPC, who was a round white faceless character, PS1 style, and a woman, also in PS1 style, stood next to me. Turning around, I saw a man watching on his old computer screen an Omori-like interface, with 4 characters, one of them missing—me. The 3 characters had to spam a B button. And I was the missing one needed to fully fill the bar.

~

Wandering through the hallways, I ended up in front of a strange wall with tiles. I clicked on several tiles and they revealed patterns. I eventually unlocked a video, in the style of a 1980s cartoon, featuring a pale-skinned man with big teeth and long golden hair. He was giving a speech about despair and the futility of human life.

Dream map.
