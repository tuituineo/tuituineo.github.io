April 16, 2025 – Car, Grudge, and Understanding

I was getting out of a car, in what looked like the parking/garden area of a house. I wasn’t supposed to go directly to the house attached to the garden, but to another one a bit further away, as if it were down a kind of driveway. However, I knew I was in "enemy" territory. It was Gabriel's parents’ house. The people with me were unpacking their things, and I was near the trunk, hiding my face in a piece of fabric. Maybe they wouldn’t recognize me? I thought it was a bit ridiculous — surely they’d recognize me by my body shape or my walk at least. So I decided to accept it, and as I revealed myself, @Gabriel’s_mom had a grim expression. Full of hatred. She clearly didn’t appreciate that I had become her son’s ex. As if I was the source of all his problems. She shook my hand tightly and walked away, staring at me looooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooong and hard in the eyes. Then @Gabriel’s_dad came to see me. He was not on the same wavelength at all. He asked how I was doing and if I was okay. I talked to him as if he were the same person from the dream on 04/15/25. He also asked me if I could stay friends with his son, because he was worried about him and his mental health. I told him I wasn’t the one against the idea, since I had already tried to rekindle a friendship, but it hadn’t been well received.

I really cried.
