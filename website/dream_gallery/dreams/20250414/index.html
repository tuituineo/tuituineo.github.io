April 14, 2025 – Dig, Mom, and DIY

It was my first initiation day to "Dig", a kind of sport where you had to dig. It was my mom who was showing me the ropes. At one point, I was in her old bedroom and on the bed, there was some kind of tool. It was one of the required items for Dig. I was wondering how it worked. There were two kinds of carabiners with stakes. Mom arrived and talked to me, but I don’t remember what she said.

The first tip was for fabric and was packaged like a DIY item.

The second tip was for the ground, with several small hooks.
