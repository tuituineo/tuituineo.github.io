May 24, 2023 – School, Christian Religion, and Calm

...I had to go to a school for my internship. It was located downhill in a snowy mountain. While walking, I passed a church that looked somewhat abandoned. When I arrived, I found myself in a classroom with a teacher. It was chaos — a Friday afternoon and no student was listening. At one point, one of them had a panic attack, which threw everyone into a frenzy. Two people climbed onto the ceiling, others were screaming, and even the teacher was curled up on the floor, reliving intense and painful memories related to her husband. I took charge and asked everyone to be quiet and sit down calmly, using my deep voice. I asked them to settle down and close their eyes. I began guiding them through a hypnosis-like visualization to help them relax. It kind of worked. Among the students, there was Sans with two big glowing blue eyes. Next to him was his realistic double, also with blue eyes, which I accidentally knocked over.

At one point, I saw a fight in the snow between a tiny demon and another person. The demon nearly died, but I rescued him and brought him back to the church from earlier. I had a conversation with the priest, but I don’t remember it. ...I was living with mom in the house, and across the street there was a church. People were going in, but mom didn’t like it because it was Audrey’s gift. I thought it was a great spot for a photoshoot.
