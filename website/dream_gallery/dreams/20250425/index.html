25 April 2025 - Improvisation, Bad and Sandra

I've dreamt...

It's rare that dreams directly echo what I'm thinking, what I'm experiencing so clearly, while also "predicting" my worries. It's quite astonishing.

I dreamed we were at an improv show, on a stage bigger than the university campus but with the same audience. It wasn't a classic improv show, but a show with Cub Scouts and only a few experienced players. It was, honestly, a disaster. The lack of listening, the topics, the improvisations that had neither queue nor purpose. The audience was bored; some were on their tablets or phones. To close the show, we sang a song about spaghetti. It was awful. After the show ended, I could hear the players complaining about the show. When we left the venue, we were in a sort of store/apartment at Clara's, where I had gone to undress behind a fence. A kid was passing by, and Sandra was talking to a guy. She saw the kid and said, "You shouldn't look at people when they're undressing; it's not nice." The child left and Sandra continued her conversation nearby. I heard her say, "I've known love," as she turned to me. I blushed and exclaimed, "Hey!" which could mean either not watching me change, or something.

This probably represents some concerns I have about the upcoming Cubs vs. South game. I don't know if I should bring it up. A little afraid people will think I'm a dream guru or something...
