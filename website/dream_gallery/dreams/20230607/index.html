June 7, 2023 – Child, Violence, and Cosplay

...Seb and I had to go to a party near downtown Saint-Denis. We left by car, and on the way we passed Blondie’s house—she was chilling on her balcony, cosplaying as Wednesday. I told Seb I was a bit upset because I thought her cosplay party was supposed to be the next day, but apparently it was tonight and we were going to miss it. But we had no choice—family dinner came first. We arrived and Audrey was there with Hayden and some others. The party was in full swing, but Audrey was arguing with someone. I went outside because I didn’t feel comfortable. Sitting outside, leaning against the house wall, Hayden joined me. “Nice, huh?” I asked ironically. “Yeah…” he replied, then explained that he went through that every day and was suffering from it. I was surprised—he spoke so much more and so much better than before. He used to pretend to speak like a toddler, but in reality, that wasn’t the case. Marine and Audrey came to get us—one was panicked, the other annoyed. I took off running, bolting down the sidewalks. Seb tried to catch up with me. At some point during the night, I saw pirate shuttles coming out of the moon and heading toward us. That’s when I realized I was dreaming and decided to use powers, just for fun.
